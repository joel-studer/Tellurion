# Product design audit (2026-09-16)

Scored from the screenshots in `docs/screenshots/`, captured from the running
demo on this machine after the rebrand, and from the measured benchmark in
[`world-renderer-decision.md`](world-renderer-decision.md). Nothing here is
carried over from an earlier round: each axis was re-scored by opening the
image and looking for what is wrong with it.

Images actually inspected for this pass: `tellurion-hero.png`, `landing.png`,
`gallery.png`, `time-machine.png`, `evidence-drawer.png`. Axes that depend on
images not inspected in this pass are marked as such rather than given a
confident number.

## Scores

| Axis | Score | Why this number and not a higher one |
|---|---|---|
| Brand identity | 8.0 | The mark (sunlit sphere, terminator, tilted orbit) is original, consistent across header, landing and gallery, and avoids every cliché on the banned list. It is not yet distinctive enough at 16 px, and the wordmark is set in the body typeface rather than anything proprietary. |
| First-screen wow | 9.0 | The hero is genuinely arresting: a sunlit globe with a real terminator, aircraft and vessel glyphs, cluster badges, a storm track and a satellite swath, with the evidence drawer already open on a real story. It earns the first screen. |
| Globe quality | 8.5 | Real sphere, correct day/night from the world clock, atmospheric limb, clean coastlines, legible clustering. Held back by flat shading: no bathymetry or relief, so the oceans are a single value and the globe reads slightly papery. |
| Map quality (zoomed in) | 6.0 | **The weakest axis.** At Level 2 the 1:50m Natural Earth geography is too coarse for a city-scale view: land/sea contrast collapses, the coast reads as a soft grey shape, and the weather cells render as pale smudges without definition. Honest gap, not a rendering bug. |
| Typography | 8.5 | Inter throughout, a clear and consistent scale, nothing below 10.5 px, good numeric alignment in the monospace fields. Metadata rows in the drawer are dense and could breathe more. |
| Information hierarchy | 8.0 | Left nav groups (World / Movement / Earth / Weather / Infrastructure / Intelligence) are legible, and the scenario banner is unmissable. The top bar carries six competing stat clusters — world time, mode, sources, objects, system — which is the busiest part of the screen. |
| Evidence UX | 8.5 | Every object answers the same questions in the same order; `Modeled` vs `Reported` chips are consistently applied; each row carries layer, time and source. One real defect: the last evidence card is clipped mid-sentence at the panel edge in every capture. |
| Time machine UX | 9.0 | Three explicit modes, tick markers, speed controls, a `Now` button, and a plain-language line stating what belief mode is doing ("only what was known by T+6m. Later reports stay hidden…"). This is the clearest part of the product. |
| Landing | 8.5 | Value proposition in one line, a real hero capture with a caption saying it is synthetic, the specified CTAs, and the three-command quickstart above the fold. Below the fold it is text-heavy: four prose sections with only one card row between them. |
| Gallery | 8.0 | Nine cards bind to real data, verified: 20 cards rendered, zero empty, no `undefined`. Rights, network and secrets are stated per plugin. Card heights are ragged because descriptions differ in length, and the registry section below is long and unfiltered. |
| README hook | 8.0 | Correct brand, a strong hero image, capability chips and the exact three-command quickstart, with licence status stated honestly up top. Scored after fixing a broken hero-GIF reference found during this audit. |
| Shareability | 7.5 | Social cards and favicon rasters are generated by the capture harness, but they were missing entirely until this pass — `IDENTITY.md` had been describing files that did not exist. Now generated; not yet reviewed at real posting sizes. |
| Performance | 8.0 | 59.5–59.9 FPS at the 95th-percentile frame from hero through 10 000 objects, selection at 38–42 ms, toggle at 26–33 ms. Two honest deductions: the mean frame rate sits near 49 because of occasional 60–80 ms frames, and startup stretches to 4.1 s at 10k. |
| Trust | 9.5 | The strongest axis. Every surface labels the data synthetic, the storm carries "not a forecast", confidence reads "Not scored (synthetic demo)", the narrative says "Editorial template written for this demo, not an AI summary", the fictional place is marked fictional, cameras say "never people", and the release gates report NOT_RUN / NOT_OBTAINED rather than a green light. |

**Overall product design: 8.3 / 10.**

That is below the 9.5 aspiration and the number is deliberate. The product
clears 9 on wow, time machine and trust, but the zoomed-in map quality (6.0)
and shareability (7.5) are genuinely unfinished, and an average that ignored
them would be flattery rather than an audit.

## Defects found by looking at the images

1. **Evidence drawer clips its last card.** In every capture the final
   evidence row is cut mid-sentence at the panel boundary, which reads as an
   unfinished layout rather than a scroll affordance.
2. **Coarse geography at high zoom.** Natural Earth 1:50m is the right trade
   for an offline globe and the wrong resolution for Level 2. The local view
   pays for the offline guarantee.
3. **Count disagreement in the hero.** The top chip row and the left nav report
   different aircraft counts in the same frame (89 vs 80). One of the two is
   counting something the other is not; from a screenshot alone it is not
   possible to say which is correct, so this is recorded as an observation to
   chase, not a diagnosed bug.
4. **Broken asset references.** The README pointed at a hero GIF, and
   `IDENTITY.md` at favicon and social PNGs, that had never been generated.
   Found here, generated, and re-checked.
5. **Occasional long frames.** The mean-vs-p95 gap in the benchmark is a real
   stutter during sustained drag, not a measurement artefact.

6. **Two screenshots are near-duplicates.** `evidence-drawer.png` and
   `time-machine.png` are the same Level 2 frame with the same drawer open;
   only the mode chip differs. The capture plan implies more variety than the
   images deliver, and the evidence shot does not actually showcase the drawer.

None of these is fixed by adjusting a score. Items 1–3, 5 and 6 remain open.

## What was verified rather than assumed

- Gallery and landing were loaded headless and asserted to contain no
  `undefined`, `NaN`, `null` or `[object Object]`, with zero page errors and
  zero failed requests; the gallery rendered 20 cards, none empty.
- The gallery renders only fields `/api/plugins` actually returns; a test now
  fails if the page invents a health or status field the API does not supply.
- The `/ultra/classic` fallback is now covered by a regression test, because it
  was silently broken: its assets 404'd and Leaflet never loaded.
