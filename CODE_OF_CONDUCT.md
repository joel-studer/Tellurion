# Contributor Covenant — lightweight adoption (preview)

Tellurion aims at a factual, review-first culture: argue about evidence,
rights, and interfaces — not about people.

## Promise

A harassment-free contribution experience for everyone, regardless of
background. Reviewers explain FAILs with file, line, and fix.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, publishing private data, submitting data
you have no right to redistribute, or smuggling credential material
into plugins, fixtures, or logs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may hide, revert, or refuse contributions that violate the
trust model or rights policy. Reports go to the security contact in
`SECURITY.md` (placeholder until published).

Full text of the Contributor Covenant: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/
(adopted in spirit for the preview; version pin at 1.0).
