World

A globe you can interrogate

Spin the Earth, fly into a region, and watch aggregated counts resolve into individual aircraft, vessels, satellite passes, and weather. Day and night are modeled from the world clock. Geography is Natural Earth, public domain: no tiles are fetched and no terrain is invented.

Evidence

Every object can be questioned

Select anything and the drawer answers the same questions in the same order: what happened, which sources said so and when, what corroborates it, which entities are related, where coverage is thin, and what the rights are. Reported, modeled and corroborated are labelled, never blurred.

Sources, not vibesEach report carries its layer, its time, and its observation state.
Blind spotsThin coverage is drawn on the map. Missing data is never shown as calm.
Rights firstLicence and data rights travel with every record and every plugin.

Time

What did we know, and when?

The Time Machine scrubs the replay minute by minute in three modes: the current world, the historical belief at a chosen moment, and the current replay. In belief mode later reports stay hidden until the clock reaches them, so hindsight cannot leak into the picture.

Plugins

Add a sensor in an afternoon

A sensor plugin is a manifest, a fixture, a poll(), and a test. The validator refuses network calls, credential tokens, and imports from outside the core, so a plugin can be reviewed by reading it.
